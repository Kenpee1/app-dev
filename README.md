## My Favorite Movie: John Carter (2012)

*John Carter* is a **sci-fi adventure** film directed by **Andrew Stanton** and based on *A Princess of Mars* by Edgar Rice Burroughs.

> A Civil War veteran is mysteriously transported to Mars and gets caught up in a war between its kingdoms.

### Main Cast

- Taylor Kitsch as John Carter
- Lynn Collins as Dejah Thoris
- Willem Dafoe as Tars Tarkas
- Mark Strong as Matai Shang

### Why I Like It

1. The visuals and world-building are impressive.
2. The score by Michael Giacchino is great.
3. It has a strong cult following despite its box office results.
